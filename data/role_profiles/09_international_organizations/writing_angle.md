# Writing angle

## Recommended CV titles
- Data Analyst and Economist
- Junior Policy Analyst, Data and Sustainability
- International Policy Data Analyst

## Profile paragraph angle
Present Yannis as an internationally oriented data analyst and economist with quantitative, policy and sustainability experience.

## Cover letter opening angle
Open on the link between international policy work, reliable quantitative evidence and his applied data background.

## Greenly framing
Frame Greenly as transferable sustainability data experience with methodology, reporting and non-technical communication.

## DataForGood / Reclaim Finance framing
Frame DataForGood / Reclaim Finance as public-interest policy data work on territorial vulnerability and climate adaptation.

## Academic projects framing
Frame the thesis, portfolio and ML project as evidence of cross-country analysis, applied economics and quantitative methods.

## Avoid
- Do not overstate direct international organization experience.
- Do not use French public-sector wording by default.
- Do not make the profile too local if the role is international.
