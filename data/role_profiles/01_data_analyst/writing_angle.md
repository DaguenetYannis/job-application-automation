# Writing angle

## Recommended CV titles
- Data Analyst junior
- Data Analyst SQL, Python et reporting
- Data Analyst ESG et qualit- des donn-es

## Profile paragraph angle
Position Yannis as a junior data analyst who turns heterogeneous operational, ESG and public datasets into reliable indicators, clean tables and usable reporting outputs.

## Cover letter opening angle
Open with the match between reliable data work and his experience cleaning, structuring and documenting data at Greenly and DataForGood.

## Greenly framing
Frame Greenly as applied data analysis on carbon and ESG datasets: SQL, Python, Excel, Google Sheets, validation checks, emission factors and client-ready reporting.

## DataForGood / Reclaim Finance framing
Frame DataForGood / Reclaim Finance as public-interest data work on a French municipal fiscal dataset, with Python, DuckDB, quality checks and territorial indicators.

## Academic projects framing
Use academic projects as supporting evidence for quantitative maturity, visualization and applied analysis.

## Avoid
- Do not make the profile look like a data engineer.
- Do not imply senior BI or production ML ownership.
- Do not list every project if the job is operational.
