# Writing angle

## Recommended CV titles
- Consultant junior secteur public et data
- Analyste conseil secteur public
- Consultant junior politiques publiques et analytics

## Profile paragraph angle
Present Yannis as a junior consultant profile with data, policy and stakeholder communication skills.

## Cover letter opening angle
Open on his ability to structure analyses, produce indicators and explain results for operational decisions.

## Greenly framing
Frame Greenly as client-facing restitution, methodology explanation, dashboards and operational reporting.

## DataForGood / Reclaim Finance framing
Frame DataForGood / Reclaim Finance as mission-driven analytics and collaboration on public-policy indicators.

## Academic projects framing
Use academic projects as proof of structured reasoning, synthesis and sector knowledge.

## Avoid
- Do not sound purely academic.
- Do not drown the letter in tools.
- Do not imply senior consulting experience.
