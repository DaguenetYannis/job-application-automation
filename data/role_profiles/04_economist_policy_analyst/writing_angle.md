# Writing angle

## Recommended CV titles
- -conomiste junior et analyste politiques publiques
- Analyste -conomique quantitatif
- Charg- d'-tudes -conomiques junior

## Profile paragraph angle
Position Yannis as an economist with strong applied data skills, able to connect quantitative evidence, economic reasoning and policy questions.

## Cover letter opening angle
Open on the fit between evidence-based policy analysis and his training in economics, econometrics and applied data work.

## Greenly framing
Frame Greenly as applied economic and environmental data work, especially carbon data, methodology and communication.

## DataForGood / Reclaim Finance framing
Frame DataForGood / Reclaim Finance as public-policy analysis grounded in fiscal and territorial indicators.

## Academic projects framing
Frame the thesis, CBAM note and portfolio as the main evidence of economic analysis and policy synthesis.

## Avoid
- Do not make the letter read as a pure data analyst application.
- Do not claim senior economist publications.
- Do not overstate macroeconomic modelling.
