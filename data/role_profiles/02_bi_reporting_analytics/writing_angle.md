# Writing angle

## Recommended CV titles
- Data Analyst BI junior
- Analyste reporting et Power BI
- Data Analyst KPI et tableaux de bord

## Profile paragraph angle
Present Yannis as a BI-oriented analyst who prepares reliable datasets, designs indicators and makes reporting understandable for users.

## Cover letter opening angle
Open on clean data models, clear KPIs and dashboards that help teams make decisions.

## Greenly framing
Frame Greenly as reporting and restitution work on carbon indicators, with data preparation, validation and communication to non-technical users.

## DataForGood / Reclaim Finance framing
Frame DataForGood / Reclaim Finance as Power BI prototyping and territorial indicator design from structured fiscal datasets.

## Academic projects framing
Use portfolio visualizations and ML work as evidence of dashboard logic, model comparison and analytical communication.

## Avoid
- Do not overstate advanced DAX.
- Do not make economics the main story unless the job mentions policy or sector analysis.
- Do not imply senior BI architecture responsibility.
