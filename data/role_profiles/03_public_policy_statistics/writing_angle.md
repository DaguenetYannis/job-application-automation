# Writing angle

## Recommended CV titles
- Charg- d'-tudes statistiques junior
- Data analyst politiques publiques
- Analyste donn-es territoriales et indicateurs publics

## Profile paragraph angle
Present Yannis as a data analyst and economist able to produce robust indicators, document methods and support public decision-making.

## Cover letter opening angle
Open on reliable statistical evidence for public action and connect it to his territorial and climate data work.

## Greenly framing
Frame Greenly as methodological and data-quality experience on carbon indicators, useful for reliable statistical production.

## DataForGood / Reclaim Finance framing
Frame DataForGood / Reclaim Finance as the central experience: municipal fiscal data, territorial indicators, quality checks and public-policy interpretation.

## Academic projects framing
Frame the thesis and portfolio as applied quantitative economics with policy relevance.

## Avoid
- Avoid private-sector sales language.
- Avoid too much dashboard vocabulary if the role is institutional statistics.
- Avoid claiming civil-service experience.
