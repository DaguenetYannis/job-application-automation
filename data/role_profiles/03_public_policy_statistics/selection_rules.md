# Selection rules

## Select this profile when
- Charg- d'-tudes statistiques
- Data analyst in ministry
- Public statistical service
- Dares, DEPP, SSM, INSEE-adjacent roles
- Territorial analysis
- Public indicators and evaluation

## Do not select this profile when
- private-sector sales analytics
- pure BI reporting without policy content
- pure climate consulting without public statistics
