# Writing angle

## Recommended CV titles
- Data Analyst climat et ESG
- Analyste transition bas-carbone
- Analyste donn-es climat et durabilit-

## Profile paragraph angle
Present Yannis as a data analyst and economist who combines carbon data, ESG reporting, climate policy and quantitative analysis.

## Cover letter opening angle
Open on the role of reliable data in climate transition decisions and connect it directly to Greenly and his climate economics projects.

## Greenly framing
Frame Greenly as the anchor: carbon and ESG datasets, scopes, emission factors, GHG Protocol, SBTi, BEGES, FLAG, validation and restitution.

## DataForGood / Reclaim Finance framing
Frame DataForGood / Reclaim Finance as climate adaptation and territorial vulnerability analysis from public fiscal data.

## Academic projects framing
Frame the thesis and CBAM note as evidence of climate transition, industrial decarbonation and economic-policy reasoning.

## Avoid
- Do not claim senior climate strategy ownership.
- Do not present field ecology expertise.
- Do not dilute climate relevance with generic BI details.
