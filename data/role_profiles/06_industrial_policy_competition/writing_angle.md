# Writing angle

## Recommended CV titles
- -conomiste politique industrielle junior
- Analyste politique industrielle et transition bas-carbone
- Analyste -conomique concurrence et r-gulation

## Profile paragraph angle
Present Yannis as an economist interested in industrial policy, value chains, competitiveness and low-carbon transition, supported by quantitative methods.

## Cover letter opening angle
Open on the need to connect economic evidence, regulation and transition challenges in industrial policy decisions.

## Greenly framing
Frame Greenly as practical carbon and sector-data experience, useful for understanding decarbonation constraints and reporting.

## DataForGood / Reclaim Finance framing
Frame DataForGood / Reclaim Finance as public-interest quantitative work and indicator construction.

## Academic projects framing
Frame the thesis and CBAM note as central evidence: value chains, industrial transition, competitiveness and climate instruments.

## Avoid
- Do not claim direct competition authority experience.
- Do not overdo technical data tooling.
- Do not frame the application as generic sustainability.
